Services

Six disciplines, one way of working. Every project follows the same five steps, so you always know what comes next.

Architecture

Houses and small buildings, including floating homes. We shape the volume, the plan and the facades together with the interior, so the building and the rooms inside it are one design.

What you receiveConcept and massing · Plans, sections and elevations · Facades and materials · Documentation for permits · 3D visualisation · Coordination with engineers and builders

Interior design

Private homes, apartments and small hospitality spaces. We plan the layout, choose the materials and draw the custom furniture and joinery, then follow the work on site.

What you receiveLayout and space planning · Materials and finishes · Custom joinery and furniture · Lighting plan · Technical drawings · Site supervision

Yacht interior design

Cabins, saloons and full refits for motor and sailing yachts. Within the limits of weight, curves and class rules, we design interiors that feel calm and generous in a small volume.

What you receiveLayout studies · Materials and soft furnishings · Joinery details · Lighting · Renders for owner approval · Coordination with the shipyard

Product and industrial design

Furniture, lighting and objects, from the first sketch to a model ready for production. We design with the maker in mind, so the object can really be built.

What you receiveConcept sketches · 3D CAD models · Prototypes and samples · Production drawings · Product renders

3D visualisation and rendering

Photorealistic images of interiors, yachts and products, for our own projects and for architects, developers, shipyards and brands.

What you receiveInterior renders · Yacht and exterior renders · Product images · 360° views · Short animations

Branding and graphic design

Identities, printed matter and signage, often for the same places we design. A café, a showroom or a boat can share one visual language, from the logo to the menu.

What you receiveLogo and identity · Typography and colour · Print and stationery · Signage · Packaging

How we work

Bit by bit, whether it is a cabin, a kitchen or a lamp.

  1. I

    Brief

    We meet in person or online. We listen to how you live, work or sail, and together we agree on scope, timing and budget.

  2. II

    Concept

    Mood boards, first sketches and a layout. One clear idea before any detail.

  3. III

    Design

    Materials, furniture, light and technical drawings. Everything decided, measured and specified.

  4. IV

    Visualisation

    Photorealistic renders of the design, so you can see the space and adjust it while changes are still easy.

  5. V

    Delivery

    Drawings for builders, shipyards or manufacturers, and follow-up until the work is finished.
